MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by Summit Peak Manufacturing ("Customer") and Brightline Data Corp. ("Vendor").

1. SERVICES
1.1 Vendor shall provide the document management and support services described in Exhibit A (the "Services") to Customer.

2. FEES
2.1 Customer shall pay Vendor $20,000 per month, invoiced monthly and due within thirty (30) days.

3. TERM
3.1 This Agreement begins on March 1, 2027 and continues for an initial term of 12 months.

4. RENEWAL
4.1 After the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive five (5) year periods unless either party gives at least 10 days' prior written notice of non-renewal.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
5.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement.

6. DATA PROTECTION
6.1 Vendor shall process Customer data only on Customer's documented instructions and shall notify Customer of any security incident within seventy-two (72) hours.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
7.1 EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

8. INDEMNIFICATION
8.1 Vendor shall defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the Services infringe any intellectual property right.

9. TERMINATION
9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach that remains uncured 30 days after written notice.

10. GOVERNING LAW
10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

11. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
11.1 This Agreement, including its exhibits, is the entire agreement between the parties.
