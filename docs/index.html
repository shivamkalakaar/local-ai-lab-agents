The Local AI Lab

Own Your AI Stack

A Stanford student lab building the full stack to run AI on your own computers: hardware, tuned open models, ready agents, and training that keeps them improving. Strong AI for the private work that cloud AI can't touch.

What we build

Every layer, on your hardware

We set up and run each layer of the AI stack on machines you own.

01

Hardware

We pick and set up the machines you own, from a laptop to a small server.

02

Open models

Open-weight models, chosen for your task by testing several on the same work.

03

Agents

Ready agents that run on our own harness, the code that drives the model. They look things up and check their answers.

04

Training

Your approved corrections become training data, so the models improve over time.

Principles

Our Principles

01

Open Source AI Models

Everything we build uses openly licensed models and is released as open source, open to inspect and modify.

02

Local Hardware

Our tools run entirely on hardware you own, from laptops to community servers, with no cloud subscription and no data leaving the device.

03

Open Models That Keep Improving

We build on open models and post-train them on your approved work, so agents get better over time.
